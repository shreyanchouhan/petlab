The test menu

Test what matters

44 parameters across 7 clinical profiles, supporting organ function, metabolic health and overall wellbeing.

Liver Profile

12 Parameters

Assess liver function and overall metabolic health.

  • SGOT (AST)
  • SGPT (ALT)
  • SGOT/SGPT Ratio
  • Total Protein
  • Albumin
  • Globulin
  • A/G Ratio
  • Total Bilirubin
  • Direct Bilirubin
  • Indirect Bilirubin
  • Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)
  • GGT

Kidney & Urinary Profile

8 Parameters

Assess kidney function, filtration and urinary health.

  • Urea
  • Creatinine
  • Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN)
  • BUN/Creatinine Ratio
  • Urea/Creatinine Ratio
  • Urine Creatinine
  • Microalbumin (Urine)
  • Urine ACR (uACR)

Metabolic & Minerals

5 Parameters

Monitor blood glucose, uric acid and key minerals.

  • Glucose
  • Uric Acid
  • Total Calcium
  • Phosphorus
  • Magnesium

Lipid Profile

8 Parameters

Helps assess fat metabolism and supports cardiovascular health.

  • Total Cholesterol
  • Triglycerides
  • LDL Cholesterol
  • HDL Cholesterol
  • Non-HDL Cholesterol
  • Cholesterol/HDL Ratio
  • LDL/HDL Ratio
  • Triglycerides/HDL Ratio

Electrolyte Profile

3 Parameters

Assess fluid, acid-base and electrolyte balance.

  • Sodium (Na⁺)
  • Potassium (K⁺)
  • Chloride (Cl⁻)

Pancreatic Profile

2 Parameters

Assess pancreatic function and digestive health.

  • Amylase
  • Lipase

Haematology & Iron

6 Parameters

Assess oxygen carrying capacity, iron status and inflammation.

  • Haemoglobin
  • Iron
  • UIBC
  • Total Iron Binding Capacity (TIBC)
  • Transferrin Saturation
  • C-Reactive Protein (CRP)
