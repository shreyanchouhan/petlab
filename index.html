Questions

Frequently asked

How fast is a VETCARE report?

Around 30 minutes from sample to a structured, AI-analysed report, inside a single consultation rather than the 24–48 hours a central-laboratory round trip typically takes.

What makes it "AI-powered"?

The intelligence runs on the device. It applies species-specific reference intervals automatically, auto-flags every parameter high or low, and detects anomalous readings and sample errors before they can mislead a diagnosis. Reports come out structured and shareable, in the vet's preferred language.

Which parameters does it run?

Seventeen parameters across five clinical profiles: Liver (AST, ALT, Total Protein, Albumin, Globulin, Total Bilirubin, A/G Ratio, AST/ALT Ratio), Kidney (Urea, Creatinine, BUN, BUN/Creatinine Ratio, Urea/Creatinine Ratio), Diabetic (Glucose), Lipid (Cholesterol, Triglyceride) and Haematology (Haemoglobin).

Does someone need laboratory training to run it?

No. The VETCARE Connect app guides the operator step by step, and a veterinary assistant can be fully trained in under a day.

Can it be used outside a clinic?

Yes. The unit is battery operated and packed in a single rugged case, so it runs on home visits, at mobile camps and in field settings with no fixed laboratory infrastructure.

Is it approved?

VETCARE is built on the mobilab platform, which is CDSCO licensed (MFG/IVD/2025/000042), ISO 13485 & 9001 certified and IEC ESD compliant (61000-4-2:2008 & 61000-4-8:2009), and has been validated in human diagnostics by AIIMS Delhi, the Indian Army, IIT Guwahati and GMCH Guwahati. Veterinary validation of VETCARE is in progress.

Which species is the panel validated for?

Reference intervals are applied automatically from the species recorded for the patient. The specific validated species list is not yet stated in our source documents. Please ask us directly and we will confirm.
